Lab · How every lesson works

⚗️ The five-phase cycle

Every Farvour session follows the same structure. Students always know what's coming — so they spend their energy thinking, not navigating.

1

🤔 Wonder — the hook

An unexpected question or real-world provocation before any instruction. Students write their thinking first. Curiosity before content.

2

🔍 Discover — build the model

Tap-to-reveal vocabulary, concept panels, and interactive scales. Students construct their mental framework before the investigation begins.

3

🧪 Lab — hands-on digital investigation

Interactive simulations, MCQ with instant feedback, data-sorting activities, and virtual dissections. Learning by genuinely doing.

4

📊 Analyse — make sense of results

Live score feedback, pattern recognition, and guided analysis help students see their own misconceptions in real time — not after the test.

5

💬 Reflect — thinking about thinking

Students write what surprised them, what they still wonder, and how their thinking changed. This metacognitive step is what makes it stick.
